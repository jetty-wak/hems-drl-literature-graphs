# Safe exploration in continuous action spaces

**BibTeX key:** `Dalal2018`  
**Year:** 2018  
**Author(s):** Dalal, Gal and others  
**Cited in:** Ch2, Ch3/4

## Claims supported in thesis

- Explicit projection methods enforce feasibility at every step
- Hard constraints require a different treatment: the controller must either be prevented from executing infeasible actions through projection or trained within a constrained formulation that exposes feasibility explicitly
- Soft objectives can be represented through reward shaping
