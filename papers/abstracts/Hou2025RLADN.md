## Claims supported in thesis

- The DRL energy-systems literature has produced a growing set of simulation environments
- Distribution-level environments commonly prioritise voltage regulation and network security tasks
- By contrast, dedicated ESS-dispatch environments are designed to represent storage constraints and dispatch decisions more directly
