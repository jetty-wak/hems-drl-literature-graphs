## Claims supported in thesis

- Operational scheduling and cost-minimisation formulations for residential PV--battery systems inform the inclusion criteria
- The choice of information available at decision time is therefore as important as the physical system boundary
- In HEMS benchmark studies, model-based baselines are often useful when objectives, operating constraints, and forecast inputs are stated explicitly and inspected in schedule form
- First, their performance depends strongly on forecast quality, so degraded PV or demand predictions can propagate directly into suboptimal schedules
- Third, real-time deployment becomes more demanding as horizon length, device granularity, and discrete switching decisions increase
