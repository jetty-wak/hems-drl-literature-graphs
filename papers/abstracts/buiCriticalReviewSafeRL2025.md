## Claims supported in thesis

- It is to show why a credible comparison between optimisation and DRL should make boundary assumptions, information sets, constraint handling, tariff assumptions, and KPI definitions explicit before performance differences are interpreted
- & Power and energy systems & Constraint-aware control & Safe RL review & Motivates explicit feasibility reporting beyond reward penalties
- Because tariff structures alter the behaviour that counts as good battery control, controller comparisons should test whether conclusions remain stable when the pricing rule changes and should report economic, grid-relevant, and feasibility metrics together
