## Claims supported in thesis

- The corpus primarily draws on residentially relevant EMS/DR studies that model PV, batteries, and flexible household loads under price-based or market-linked incentives
- However, these approaches become more difficult to scale under uncertainty and forecast error, which is one reason the literature has increasingly turned toward learning-based control methods that do not require the same explicit predictive structure
- In this literature, model-based baselines are typically specified through explicit rules or optimisation models
- Review literature presents DRL as an attractive option under uncertainty and system complexity
- This property is useful when the controller must operate repeatedly under varied price, load, and PV conditions
