# Optimal Sizing and Energy Scheduling of Photovoltaic-Battery Systems Under Different Tariff Structures

**BibTeX key:** `Talent_2018`  
**Cited in:** Ch2, Ch3/4

## Claims supported in thesis

- Hence, different tariff regimes do not merely change costs; they can also change what counts as good battery control
- For this reason, residential EMS studies are often more informative when they do not assess performance under only one tariff regime
- Optimisation baselines are useful because they show how schedules change under different pricing structures
- More recent tariff analyses show that dispatch incentives change materially once asymmetric feed-in compensation and post-net-metering settlement rules are introduced
- Because tariff structures alter the behaviour that counts as good battery control, controller comparisons should test whether conclusions remain stable when the pricing rule changes and should report economic, grid-relevant, and feasibility metrics together
