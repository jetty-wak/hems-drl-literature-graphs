**Key:** `Achiam2017`  
**Type:** misc

## Why cited in this thesis

- Cited as foundational Lagrangian/constrained-RL approach for hard-constraint satisfaction during DRL training, contrasted with curriculum approaches.
- Cited as Lagrangian constrained RL reference alongside projection-based safety shields (Dalal2018) in the taxonomy of hard-constraint remediation approaches.

## Thesis claims supported

- **[Ch3]** Hard constraints require a different treatment: the controller must either be prevented from executing infeasible actions through projection or trained within a constrained formulation that exposes feasibility explicitly
